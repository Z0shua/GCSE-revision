Getting a free key

Groq: sign in at console.groq.com/keys and create an API key. No card required.

Gemini: sign in at aistudio.google.com/apikey and click Create API key. No card required on the free tier.

Both free tiers are rate-limited. If a reply fails with a 429, wait a minute and try again.
